Anatomy of a Failure: The Redevelopment Project

Despite a ₹100 crore investment to revive Chandni Chowk, it transformed into an example of urban mismanagement. A detailed analysis shows how a flawed plan and bad administration caused its decline.

Core Problem

Systemic Governance Failure

Disputes between PWD & MCD lead to no accountability.

→

Direct Consequence

Total Lack of Enforcement

Traffic ban ignored, hawkers take over pavements.

→

Resulting Condition

Rapid Dilapidation

Broken tiles, overflowing garbage, chaos returns.

Underlying Philosophical Flaw

Focusing on cosmetic changes over genuine preservation, the project removed historical elements and disregarded the area's vibrant, informal economic activity.
